Understanding Word Embeddings: Building Word2Vec from Scratch

Word embeddings are foundational to modern NLP, from search engines to large language models. This notebook provides a complete implementation to understand how they work.

Key concepts covered:

SKIP-GRAM ARCHITECTURE: Predict context words from a center word. Learn why this creates meaningful representations.

NEGATIVE SAMPLING: Efficient training by contrasting positive examples with random negative samples.

DIMENSIONALITY REDUCTION: Visualize high-dimensional embeddings using:
- PCA (implemented from scratch)
- t-SNE for local structure

SEMANTIC SIMILARITY: Use cosine similarity to find related words automatically.

WORD ANALOGIES: The famous "king - man + woman = queen" property emerges from training.

The implementation uses only NumPy, making every step transparent. The notebook includes 4 visualizations showing training progress, embedding spaces, similarity matrices, and analogy relationships.

Essential knowledge for anyone working with NLP or wanting to understand how LLMs represent language.
